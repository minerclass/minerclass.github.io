    The Pouch and the Bypass.

    Four lenses on the screen debate meet one practical question: what changes when schools regulate phones, Chromebooks, platforms, and instructional screen use?

    Explore the companion Read Micah's blog Speaking and consulting
    A phone pouch changes one channel of access. School-issued devices, platforms, instructional design, and unequal conditions still shape the screen ecology around it.

    The vocabulary is traveling.

    In July 2026, EdSurge published a practitioner account of one school's response to generative AI and reached for unproductive success to name what it was seeing. Worth noting less as press than as evidence: a concept built for the qualifying paper turned out to be useful to educators describing their own classrooms.

    When the Output Looks Like Learning The source of record. EdSurge cites the work under its qualifying-paper title, Beyond Secondary Orality; it was published in i.e.: inquiry in education, 18(1), Article 4.
